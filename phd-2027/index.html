    Research Proposal研究计划书

    PDF · 5 pages · 282 KBPDF · 5 页 · 282 KB

    Design-Based Sensitivity Analysis for Matched Observational Studies under Hidden Bias, Population Shift, and Model-Generated Measurements

    Extends the propensity-anchored sensitivity analysis of matched studies to three ways real data depart from the ideal. Aim 1: how robust covariate-adaptive full-matching designs can be made to hidden bias, and which weighted contrasts to use. Aim 2: hidden bias and a shift to a different target population, treated together. Aim 3: tests that stay valid when covariates or outcomes are produced by language models, using a small human-checked calibration sample. Planned applications in clinical and science-of-science data.

    将以倾向得分为锚点的匹配研究敏感性分析，推广到实际数据偏离理想假设的三种情形。目标一：协变量自适应完全匹配设计对隐藏偏倚的稳健性能提升到何种程度，以及应采用哪种加权对比；目标二：联合处理隐藏偏倚与目标总体的偏移；目标三：当协变量或结果由语言模型生成时，借助少量人工核验的校准样本构造仍然有效的检验。计划应用于临床数据与科学学（science of science）数据。

      Causal Inference for Observational Studies观察性研究的因果推断

      Nov 2025 – present2025年11月至今

      Department of Statistics · The University of Chicago芝加哥大学统计系

      Mentor: Prof. Xinran Li导师：Xinran Li 教授

      A covariate-adaptive sensitivity analysis with a studentized test of Neyman’s weak null for matched designs, anchored at fitted propensity odds, with a Horvitz–Thompson lower envelope and an extension to full matching. On the RHC (right heart catheterization) data it gives a sensitivity value of Λ = 1.40 (uniform analysis: 1.30). Working paper, co-first-authored with Pengyun Wang.

      针对匹配观察性研究中 Neyman 弱原假设的协变量自适应学生化敏感性分析：以拟合的倾向得分比值（propensity odds）为锚点，构造 Horvitz–Thompson 下包络，并推广到完全匹配（full matching）。在右心导管（RHC）研究中，敏感性值为 Λ = 1.40（均匀敏感性分析为 1.30）。工作论文，与 Pengyun Wang 并列第一作者。
